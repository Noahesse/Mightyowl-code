Privacy Policy — Blackjack Coach

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Blackjack Coach ("the app") is developed by Mighty Blackjack Coach ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data we collect

Locally on your device only (never sent to us):

  • Your hand history, win/loss/push statistics, and settings (language, light/dark mode, surrender preference). This data is stored in your device's local app storage and is never transmitted anywhere. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes it permanently.

Via third-party services:

  • Advertising (Google AdMob): The app shows banner ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your advertising ID and other data to show and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses data in Play.
  • Purchases (Google Play Billing): If you buy the "Remove Ads" upgrade, the purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details; we only receive confirmation that the purchase was completed, which is stored locally on your device to keep ads turned off.

Data we do NOT collect

We do not operate our own servers, do not create user accounts, and do not collect your name, email, or location directly. We do not sell your data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices

  • You can remove ads permanently via the in-app "Remove Ads" purchase.
  • You can clear your local hand history and statistics at any time from the app's menu.
  • You can uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.

Changes

We may update this policy if the app's data practices change. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the latest revision.
